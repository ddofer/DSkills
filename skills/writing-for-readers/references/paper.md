# Papers

Extra rules for a paper; the rules in `SKILL.md` still apply.

- **Sections and headings.** Use the venue's standard sections and short noun headings. A paragraph heading names its topic ("Consistency"), not its claim.
- **Opening.** Say what you introduce, show and find in the first paragraph, then list "Our contributions are…". Keep the rest of the introduction short. Figure 1 shows the main idea plainly.
- **Abstract.** Keep to the venue's word limit and count it. The findings come with the number that matters most; end on a plain claim, not a stack of numbers.
- **Captions.** The first sentence says what is shown; no claim lead and no bold lead. The text, not the caption, makes the argument.
